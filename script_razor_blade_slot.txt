That tiny slot inside an old medicine cabinet leads to nowhere - or so it seems. Decades ago, replaceable safety-razor blades were common, but throwing a sharp blade into household trash could injure someone. So cabinet makers added a disposal slot. You pushed the used blade through, and it dropped into the empty space between the wall studs. In many homes, there was no box to empty and no plan to retrieve it. A wall cavity could hold years of blades. That's why renovators sometimes remove an old bathroom cabinet and discover a pile of rusty metal hidden behind the mirror.
